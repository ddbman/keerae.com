About the game

Each stage is short, so a run takes a minute or two and a faster one is always one more try away. A clock starts the moment you move. Beat it for up to three stars, find the hidden diamond and race your own ghost. Get gold on a stage and Mister Dinguz shows up to race you for a crown.

The seven worlds run from the Meadow Isles down Below the Clouds, through quicksand and tides, snow that makes SQUISH bigger, a castle that moves on the beat, walls that turn into floors and a dark old ground where SQUISH's own light shows the way. Each ends with a boss. Four moves turn up along the way: Slam, Glide, Magnet and Glow, and each opens paths in the worlds you already played.

Features

  • Seven worlds and 67 stages, with 12 secret stages and a Remix for each of the first six worlds
  • Seven bosses, each playing by its own rules
  • A daily challenge: one stage and one twist a day, the same for everyone
  • Splits, a ghost of your best run, World Runs and a Golden Run with no deaths
  • Harbor Isle, the island between the worlds, with neighbors to meet and a lap to race
  • Assist options: a slower game, more hearts, guide rails, or skip a stage that has you stuck
